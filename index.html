About HelloWord

HelloWord is a vocabulary app for kids. Type any word, or tap Surprise me!, to learn what kind of word it is, which language it came from, what it means, and how to use it in a sentence.

Common questions

A word isn't found. What should I do?

Check the spelling and try one word at a time. Some very new or very rare words aren't in the dictionary yet.

It says "No internet right now"

HelloWord looks up words online, so it needs Wi-Fi or cellular data. Connect to the internet and try again.

I can't hear the words read aloud

Turn up the media volume with the side buttons while HelloWord is open. Read-aloud works even when the iPhone is in Silent mode.

How do I clear the list of words my child explored?

On the home screen, tap Clear next to "Your words". The list is stored only on the device.

Where does the word information come from?

Meanings and word origins come from Wiktionary contributors (CC BY-SA 4.0), through the free Dictionary API. Full credits are in the app under the i button.
