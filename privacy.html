Our promise

HelloWord is a vocabulary app for children. We built it to collect as little as possible.

What we collect

Nothing. HelloWord has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect names, email addresses, locations, device identifiers or any other personal information.

Words your child looks up

To show a word's meaning and origin, the word that is typed (or picked by "Surprise me!") is sent over a secure connection to these free public services:

  • Free Dictionary API (dictionaryapi.dev)
  • Wiktionary (en.wiktionary.org)
  • Datamuse (api.datamuse.com)
  • Random Word API (random-word-api.herokuapp.com)

Only the word itself is sent. It is not linked to your child or device by HelloWord, and HelloWord does not store it on any server. These services may keep standard web server logs under their own privacy policies.

Stored on the device

HelloWord remembers the last few words your child explored so they can revisit them. This list stays on the device, is never sent anywhere, and can be cleared anytime with the "Clear" button. Deleting the app removes it.

Children's privacy

HelloWord is designed for children and follows the Apple App Store Kids Category rules: no third-party advertising or analytics, no in-app purchases, and no links that leave the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
